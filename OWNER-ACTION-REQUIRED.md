# Owner evidence required before adding further trust claims

The optimized website keeps the current business claims but does not invent evidence. Provide the following owner-verified material before the next trust update:

1. Registered business name and complete postal address, including postcode.
2. Three to six genuine factory photographs with captions, facility name, location, date, and permission to publish.
3. Two or three client outcomes with the product, quantity or range, delivery result, measurable outcome, timeframe, and permission to publish. Anonymous case studies may be used when required by an NDA.
4. For every certification logo: certificate or audit number, certified facility, scope, issuing body, issue date, expiry date, and public verification URL where available.
5. Written confirmation for the displayed capacity, factory-network, and years-of-experience figures.

Do not publish client names, buyer logos, factory photographs, certificate numbers, addresses, or results without the relevant permission and accuracy check.
