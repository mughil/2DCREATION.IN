Fabric & finish

Specify the performance behind the look.

A fabric name alone is not enough. Composition, weight, knit structure, finishing and testing expectations should support the intended garment and market.

Fabric directionJersey, interlock, rib or another structure selected for the intended silhouette and use.
CompositionCotton, blends or other fibres considered according to performance, hand feel and commercial requirements.
Weight & recoveryDefined in relation to coverage, drape, stretch, shape retention and season.
Colour & finishShade, wash, hand feel and appearance standards aligned through approved references.
DecorationPrint, embroidery, appliqué or other technique assessed for artwork, fabric and care requirements.
TestingAny required dimensional stability, colourfastness, performance or market-specific testing must be agreed.

Development flow

From range idea to production handover.

  1. Define the wearer

    Confirm customer, market, use, season, fit direction and target quality.

  2. Select materials

    Review fabric, colour, rib, thread, trims and finish requirements.

  3. Develop the sample

    Apply measurements, construction, artwork and labelling information.

  4. Review performance

    Assess fit, appearance, workmanship and any agreed testing needs.

  5. Close approvals

    Record final sample, fabric, colour, artwork and packing decisions.

  6. Coordinate bulk

    Connect approved information to production and quality milestones.

Common questions

T-shirt sourcing basics.

What information is needed to cost a T-shirt?

Share the style, measurements, fabric composition and weight, colour, quantity, decoration, trims, labels, packing, delivery destination and required date.

Can fabric, fit and branding be customized?

Yes, subject to feasibility and order confirmation. Each element should be specified and approved through the development process.

How are print and embroidery requirements confirmed?

Provide production-ready artwork where possible, including dimensions, placement, colours and technique. A sample or strike-off approval route should be agreed.
