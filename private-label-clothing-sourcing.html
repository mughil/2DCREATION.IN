The basics

Private label, white label and custom development.

The terms are used loosely, so confirm what a supplier means before comparing quotes.

Private label, white label and custom development.
Private labelMade to your design and specification, with your own labels, hangtags and packing. Fit, fabric and trims are developed or adapted with you and approved through samples.
White labelAn existing stock design from the manufacturer with your label added. Development is limited, so the range is less distinctive.
Where 2D Creation fits2D Creation is a sourcing agent. It coordinates private label development and production with a manufacturer in Tirupur that already makes the product. It does not own the factory.

Preparation

What your brand needs to prepare.

Private label is more than a garment. The labelling, trims and packing are part of the product and need approval too.

What your brand needs to prepare.
Brand filesLogo files, colour references and label artwork in production-ready formats.
LabelsMain label, size label and care or content label. Labelling rules differ by destination market, so confirm what your market requires, for example fibre content, care instructions and country of origin.
Hangtags and stickersHangtag design, attachment method, barcodes and price tickets if required.
PackingFolding, polybag, assortment and carton marking, including any retailer packing instructions.
Tech packMeasurements, materials, construction and artwork placement for each style. See product development and sampling.
ComplianceThe safety, testing and labelling standards of your destination market. These come from the buyer and are agreed before development.

The process

How a private label order runs.

Each stage ends with a decision that is recorded, so the approved sample stays the production reference.

  1. Brief and references

    Share the product, brand direction, quantities, destination market and required dates.

  2. Factory match

    2D Creation matches your product to a manufacturer in Tirupur that already makes it, and explains why it was chosen.

  3. Development and samples

    Develop the sample, consolidate fit comments and approve fabric, colour, artwork and trims in writing.

  4. Labels and packing approval

    Approve labels, hangtags and packing on the pre-production sample, before bulk starts.

  5. Production and quality control

    Follow production against agreed milestones and inspection stages. See apparel quality control.

  6. Shipment readiness

    Confirm final packing, documents and freight coordination. See apparel sourcing in India.

Common questions

Private label clothing basics.

What is private label clothing?

Private label clothing is made to your design and specification and sold under your own brand, with your labels, hangtags and packing. It differs from white label, where a manufacturer adds your label to an existing stock design with little or no development.

Is 2D Creation a private label clothing manufacturer?

No. 2D Creation is a sourcing agent. It matches your private label brief to a manufacturer in Tirupur that already makes the product and coordinates development, sampling, production follow-up, quality control and shipment readiness. Certificates belong to the chosen factory.

Can I start without a finished tech pack?

Yes. Share the clearest references you have and mark what still needs development. The team will explain which specifications are needed before sampling or costing. See what a tech pack should contain.

Can I use my own labels, hangtags and packaging?

Yes, subject to feasibility and order confirmation. Provide label artwork, hangtag details and packing instructions, and approve them on the pre-production sample so labels and packing are checked before bulk.

What is a typical first private label order?

Typical first orders are 300 to 500 pieces per style. Minimum quantities, pricing and lead times depend on fabric, construction, decoration and packaging, and are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

Ready to brief a private label range?

Share your style references, quantities by style, colour and size, label and packing requirements, destination market and required date. If a detail is undecided, mark it clearly.
