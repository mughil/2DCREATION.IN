Private Label T-Shirts: A Practical Guide for Apparel Buyers

Private label t-shirts: how to brief, sample and quality-check your order, with 2D Creation coordinating factories in Tirupur, India. Send your brief today.

Written by the 2D Creation sourcing team in Tirupur, India, who coordinate product development, sampling, production follow-up and quality control for international apparel buyers.

If you are one of the international apparel brands and importers looking into private label t-shirts, this guide explains what to prepare, how the process works and which checks protect your order. It is written by 2D Creation, an apparel sourcing agent in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, that coordinates product development, sampling, production and quality control for international buyers.

Key takeaways

  • Start any private label t-shirts enquiry with a clear brief: product type, quantity per style and colour, target market and delivery date.
  • Approve samples in writing at each stage and keep one approved reference sample.
  • Agree the inspection standard before production starts, not after packing.
  • Ask for certificate copies, scope and validity for every factory when certification matters.

Why buyers look at Tirupur for T-shirts

Tirupur in Tamil Nadu is one of India's best-known knitwear clusters. Spinning, knitting, dyeing, printing, stitching and finishing units work close to each other, which helps knit programmes such as T-shirts, polos and casual tops move from fabric to finished garment with fewer hand-offs.

What to prepare before you ask for a quote

A clear brief saves weeks. Share the product type, target quantity per style and colour, destination market, reference samples or images, a tech pack if you have one, fabric composition and GSM, print or embroidery details, and your target delivery date.

If certification matters for your market, say which standard you need (for example OEKO-TEX or GOTS) at the start, because it narrows the factories that can be considered.

From sample to bulk production

A typical T-shirt programme moves through fabric and trim sourcing, proto or fit samples, approval samples, pre-production checks, bulk production, inline and final inspection, and shipment readiness.

Ask for written approval at each sample stage and keep one approved reference sample. Most quality disputes trace back to an unclear approval.

Read more about quality control and production follow-up.

Quality checks that matter for knit tops

For T-shirts, buyers usually focus on measurements against the size chart, shrinkage and torque after washing, shade consistency across lots, print adhesion, seam strength and neck-rib recovery.

Agree the inspection standard and the acceptable quality level before production starts, not after the goods are packed.

What private label involves in practice

Private label means the T-shirts are made to your specification and carry your brand, so the work goes beyond the garment itself. Plan these elements at the briefing stage, because each one adds a decision and a sample approval:

  • Neck labels: woven, printed or heat-transfer labels, with their size and placement on the neck.
  • Care and content labels: fibre content, care symbols, size and country of origin, in the format your destination market requires.
  • Hangtags and stickers: hangtags, barcodes and size stickers, with the artwork and the attachment method agreed.
  • Folding and packing: polybag type, carton marking and any retailer packing instructions.

Send brand artwork as production-ready files and approve a physical sample with the labels attached, not just a digital mock-up. Our typical minimum is 300 pieces per style and colour, and we confirm the exact figure for your fabric and decoration after reviewing the brief.
