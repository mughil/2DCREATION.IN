How to choose a T-shirt manufacturer in India

What decides T-shirt quality in Tirupur, which specifications to fix before you ask for prices, and how to approve a sample.

Written by the 2D Creation sourcing team in Tirupur, India, who coordinate product development, sampling, production follow-up and quality control for international apparel buyers.

Searching for a T-shirt manufacturer in India usually returns hundreds of near-identical listings. The real differences show up later: in the fabric, the sewing quality, the print durability and whether bulk matches the sample. This guide explains how T-shirt production works in Tirupur, which specifications decide cost and quality, and how to approve a sample so the bulk order does not surprise you.

Key takeaways

  • Fabric, not the factory name, decides most of a T-shirt's quality. Fix the yarn, GSM and finish before you compare prices.
  • Approve a washed sample, because shrinkage and twisting only appear after washing.
  • Ask for the print method and strike-offs on your actual fabric.
  • Agree the inspection standard and sample approval in writing before production begins.

Why Tirupur for T-shirts

Tirupur is India's largest knitwear cluster. Spinning, knitting, dyeing, printing, stitching and finishing units sit close together, so a T-shirt can move from yarn to packed carton with few hand-offs. That proximity helps with sampling speed and with small changes mid-development, such as swapping a rib or adjusting a colour.

Fit and the measurement sheet

Most disputes in T-shirt orders are fit disputes. Send a measurement sheet with points of measure, such as chest, body length, shoulder width, sleeve length and neck opening, plus tolerances. If you are copying an existing T-shirt, send the garment itself as a reference and agree which measurements are fixed and which can float.

Printing and decoration

Screen printing suits large runs and bold artwork; direct-to-garment suits small runs and detailed artwork; embroidery suits logos and premium positioning. Ask for a strike-off on your actual fabric, not a similar one, and check the print after a wash and a rub test.

Sampling: how many rounds to expect

Plan on a first sample for fit and fabric, a second for corrections and decoration, and a pre-production sample cut from the bulk fabric. Approve each in writing and keep one sealed reference sample. See how this works in product development and sampling.

Indicative cost for a T-shirt is USD 3 to 8 per piece. Final price depends on fabric, GSM, construction, trims, printing or embroidery, quantity, packaging and testing. Sampling takes 1 to 2 weeks, and the total from approval to shipment is about 8 to 10 weeks.

Quality checks before shipment

  • Fabric inspection before cutting for shade, holes and GSM.
  • Inline check during sewing for seam, neck and measurement drift.
  • Final inspection against an agreed AQL standard before packing.
  • Measurement and wash test on random pieces from the finished lot.

What to send for a quotation

Send the product type, a reference image or garment, fabric and GSM, quantity per style and colour, print or embroidery details, destination market and the date you need delivery. A buyer who is still deciding on fabric can say so, and we can propose options.

Frequently asked questions

What GSM should a T-shirt be?

Lightweight summer tees are commonly 140 to 160 GSM, standard everyday tees 160 to 200 GSM, and heavyweight or boxy streetwear tees 220 GSM and above. Choose the weight from the season, the market and the price position, then confirm it on a fabric swatch.

What is the minimum order quantity for T-shirts in Tirupur?

It depends on the fabric, whether it is dyed to your colour and the print method. Our typical MOQ is 300 pieces per style and colour. Stock-colour fabric generally allows lower quantities than custom-dyed fabric.

Which fabric is best for a premium T-shirt?

Combed or compact cotton single jersey gives a smoother surface and less pilling than carded cotton. Supima or other long-staple cotton and ring-spun yarn move the product further up the price ladder.

How do I avoid shrinkage and twisting?

Specify the shrinkage tolerance in the tech pack, ask for the fabric to be compacted and pre-shrunk, and check a washed sample before bulk. Twisting is controlled by knit structure and by how the garment is cut and sewn.

How long does a T-shirt order take?

Typical lead times are 1 to 2 weeks for sampling, 3 to 4 weeks for bulk fabric and 4 to 6 weeks for production, which is about 8 to 10 weeks in total from approval to shipment. We confirm a schedule once we have seen the brief.
