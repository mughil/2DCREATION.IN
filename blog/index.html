Turn your sourcing research into a clear order brief

Use these guides to prepare the questions and specifications your sourcing partner needs. Start with the apparel sourcing agent guide for an overview of coordination, then choose the T-shirt, sweatshirt or kidswear article that matches your range. Each product category has different fabric, fit and construction decisions to resolve before production.

Prepare your requirements before requesting a quote

Share the product type, quantity by style and colour, size range, fabric composition and weight, and any printing or embroidery requirements. Include a tech pack or reference images when available. State your delivery destination and required arrival date so sampling, production and shipping can be considered together. If a specification is undecided, identify it as a question for development.

Use samples to agree on the details

Keep a written record of fit comments, measurements, fabric choices and approved changes. Our product development and sampling service explains how a brief moves towards approval. For the next stage, review our production follow-up and quality control process.

Ready to discuss your range? Send your sourcing enquiry with your product type, quantity and delivery date. 2D Creation can review the brief and clarify the information needed for the next step.
