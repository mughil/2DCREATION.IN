Where to start

Pick the guide that matches your next order.

These guides are written for apparel buyers who are deciding how to source from Tirupur, and they build on each other. If you are still choosing a partner, begin with what an apparel sourcing agent does, then read the India sourcing guide for regions, export steps and how to check an agent before you pay anyone.

If you already know the product, go straight to the category guide: T-shirts for fabric, GSM and sampling, hoodies and sweatshirts for fleece and trims, and kidswear or babywear for safety, labels and size sets.

Whichever product you choose, read how to verify an OEKO-TEX certificate before you rely on any certification claim. When you are ready to brief a factory, see our sourcing services or send a brief with your product, quantities and delivery date.
