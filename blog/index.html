Choose a guide for your next garment order

Use these guides to prepare your sourcing brief and decide which questions to ask before placing an order. Start with the apparel sourcing agent guide if you are comparing partners or planning your first programme. For a specific product, choose the T-shirt, sweatshirt, kidswear or babywear guide. Each article covers practical steps you can discuss with your sourcing contact and factory.

Prepare a clear sourcing brief

Share the garment type, intended customer and destination market. Include a tech pack or reference images, fabric composition, fabric weight, measurements and construction details. List quantities by style, colour and size rather than providing only a total. Explain your target price, required delivery date and any labelling or packing requirements.

If some details are still undecided, identify them in the brief. This helps your sourcing partner explain which choices affect cost, sample development and production timing. A reference garment can help communicate fit and finish, but written measurements and specifications are still needed for approval.

Agree on samples and checks before production

Ask which samples are needed and who will approve them. Depending on the order, these may include development samples, fit samples, a size set and a pre-production sample. Keep written approval records and an agreed reference sample. Confirm how changes will be documented so the factory works from the latest approved specification.

Discuss inspection points, measurement tolerances, testing needs and defect criteria before bulk production. For babywear and kidswear, discuss trims, attachment strength and destination-market safety requirements. When a certificate is required, check the selected factory's current certificate, scope and validity rather than relying only on a logo.

Turn your research into the next step

Read about our product development and sampling support and production and quality coordination to understand how these stages connect. When you are ready, send your sourcing brief with your product details and reference files. 2D Creation can then review the information and discuss the next questions for your order.
