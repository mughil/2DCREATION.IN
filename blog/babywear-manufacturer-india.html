How to choose a babywear manufacturer in India

A buyer guide to newborn and infant clothing: fabric, trims, labels, packaging and testing before you place an order.

Written by the 2D Creation sourcing team in Tirupur, India, who coordinate product development, sampling, production follow-up and quality control for international apparel buyers.

Babywear sits at the sharp end of apparel sourcing. The wearer cannot say a seam is scratchy, the parent will read the label closely, and the importing country will test for chemicals and small parts. If you are a brand or retailer looking for a babywear manufacturer in India, this guide focuses on the 0 to 24 month category: the fabric, trims, labelling and testing decisions that matter before you place an order.

Key takeaways

  • Babywear is fabric-led. Choose the cotton quality and certification first.
  • Specify every trim: nickel-free snaps, secure attachment and no loose small parts.
  • Plan labels, packaging and tests as part of the product, not as an afterthought.
  • Confirm the destination market's rules before the first sample.

Fabric choices for 0 to 24 months

Most babywear uses combed cotton single jersey, rib or interlock, often in organic cotton. Softness is controlled by yarn quality, knit tightness and finish, so ask for a swatch and handle it before approving. If you need GOTS for organic claims or OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested textiles, state that in the brief, because the certification covers the supply chain and not just the final sewing.

Certificates are factory-specific, so we verify each factory's current certificate, including holder, scope and validity, before confirming an order.

Design for how babies are dressed

  • Envelope necks that stretch over the head.
  • Snaps at the crotch and shoulders for quick changes.
  • Flat or covered seams and tagless labels to reduce irritation.
  • Fold-over mitts and footed styles where needed, with no loose threads.

Trims and small parts

Snaps, buttons, zips, ribbons and appliqués need pull testing and secure attachment. Choose nickel-free metal trims, avoid anything small enough to detach, and list every trim in the tech pack. Ask for test reports on trims, not just on the finished garment.

Labelling and packaging

Babywear labels carry fibre content, care and size, and in some markets extra tracking or safety information. Decide on tagless printed labels, woven labels and hangtags early, since each adds a step to production. Specify packing too: polybag, set pack or gift box, with barcodes and retailer labelling.

Testing and compliance

Requirements vary by market. The US applies CPSIA to children's products, and the EU applies REACH limits and EN 14682 for cords and drawstrings. Confirm the specifics with an accredited lab and tell us your destination at briefing stage, so tests are scheduled before bulk production.

For a broader view of children's clothing, read kidswear sourcing in Tirupur, and see our womenswear and kidswear sourcing page.

Sampling and size sets

Babywear sizes run by age or weight, with little room for error. Approve a full size set and check neck openings, snap placement and length at each size. See product development and sampling.

Cost and timeline

Indicative cost for babywear is USD 3 to 7 per piece. Final price depends on fabric, GSM, construction, trims, printing or embroidery, quantity, packaging and testing. Sampling takes 1 to 2 weeks, and the total from approval to shipment is about 8 to 10 weeks.

Frequently asked questions

What fabric is best for newborn clothing?

Soft, breathable, lightly finished cotton such as single jersey or interlock is the usual choice. For newborns, buyers often specify organic cotton and a tested standard such as OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I, which is the strictest class and covers products for babies up to 36 months.

Are snaps safe on babywear?

They can be, if they are nickel-free, pass pull testing and sit where they do not press against the skin. Specify the trim and ask for the test report.

What labelling does babywear need?

Fibre content, care instructions, size and country of origin as a minimum, plus any destination-market requirements such as tracking labels for the US. Tagless printed labels are common to avoid itching.

What is the minimum order quantity for babywear?

Our typical MOQ is 300 pieces per style and colour. Smaller garments often have lower fabric use, but trims and printing can set the minimum.

Can you pack babywear in sets or gift boxes?

Yes. Packing is part of the brief, so tell us whether you need polybags, hangers, set packing or gift boxes, and any barcode or retailer labelling.
