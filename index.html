Quick answers

Apparel sourcing, clearly explained.

Concise facts for buyers evaluating a sourcing partner in Tirupur, India.

What is 2D Creation?

2D Creation is a global apparel sourcing partner based in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, India. The company supports international fashion brands and buyers by coordinating product development, fabric and trim sourcing, sampling, garment production, quality control, and shipping according to buyer specifications. For more information, visit 2D Creation.

2D Creation at 2dcreation.in is not an office stationery wholesaler and is not affiliated with unrelated local businesses using the same or a similar name.

What services are provided?

Services include design coordination, product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production coordination, quality control, merchandising, and shipping coordination.

What garments can be sourced?

Capabilities include men’s wear, women’s wear, kidswear, casualwear, T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, loungewear, and nightwear.

How can a buyer request a quote?

Share the product type, target quantity, delivery market, reference files, and required date through the enquiry form, email, phone, or WhatsApp.

Do I need a sourcing agent in India?

It depends on your order size, categories and whether you have a team on the ground. Read what an apparel sourcing agent does and costs and our guide to sourcing from India.

How do I specify T-shirts, hoodies or kidswear?

Fabric, GSM, trims and sampling matter most. See our guides to T-shirt manufacturing, hoodies and sweatshirts and kidswear, or browse all sourcing guides.

Is 2D Creation a garment sourcing agent or a factory?

2D Creation is a garment sourcing agent and apparel sourcing company, not a factory owner. It matches each brief to a suitable manufacturer in Tirupur, then coordinates development and sampling, production follow-up, quality control and shipment readiness. Certificates belong to the chosen factory.

Why source apparel in Tirupur, India?

Tirupur (also spelt Tiruppur) is one of India’s main knitwear clusters, known for T-shirts, hoodies, kidswear and other knitted garments, with knitting, processing, printing and garment making in the same region. Read how apparel sourcing in India works for international buyers.

Can international buyers source private label clothing through 2D Creation?

Yes. Buyers share a tech pack or references, and 2D Creation coordinates development, labels and packing, sampling, production follow-up and quality control. See private label clothing sourcing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does 2D Creation provide?

Design coordination, product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production, quality control, merchandising, and shipping coordination.

What products can you source?

Men’s wear, women’s wear, kidswear, casualwear, T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, loungewear, nightwear, and related knitted apparel.

Can garments be customized?

Yes. Fabric, colour, fit, construction, trims, prints, labels, and packaging can be developed subject to feasibility and approval.

How are pricing and minimum quantities confirmed?

They depend on product, fabric, construction, decoration, testing, packaging, quantity, and delivery requirements.

How long do sampling and production take?

Lead times vary by complexity, material availability, approval rounds, quantity, and capacity. A realistic schedule is provided after assessment.

How do I begin an enquiry?
