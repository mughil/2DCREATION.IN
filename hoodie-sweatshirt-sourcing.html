Specification guide

Heavyweight products need connected decisions.

A change in fabric, wash or decoration can affect fit, shrinkage, appearance and production method. Review the complete garment rather than approving each detail in isolation.

FabricFleece, French terry or another knit structure; define composition, weight, stretch, face and inner finish.
FitBody width, length, shoulder, sleeve, cuff and hem proportions linked to the intended customer.
HoodSingle or double layer, shape, opening, overlap, drawcord channel and lining requirements.
TrimsRib quality, zipper, eyelets, drawcord, stoppers, labels and other hardware where applicable.
DecorationArtwork, placement, technique, backing and testing selected for the fabric and garment construction.
Care performanceAgree relevant expectations for shrinkage, twisting, pilling, colour and overall appearance after care.

Common questions

Hoodie sourcing basics.

What information is needed for a hoodie enquiry?

Share the silhouette, measurements, fabric type and weight, colour, quantity, hood and pocket details, trims, decoration, labels, packing, destination and required date.

Why does fabric weight alone not define quality?

Weight is only one factor. Fibre, yarn, knit structure, finishing, hand feel, recovery, shrinkage, pilling and construction all affect the finished garment.

When should print or embroidery be tested?

Decoration should be assessed on the intended fabric and garment construction before bulk production, using an agreed approval and testing route.

Is 2D Creation a hoodie manufacturer?

No. 2D Creation is a sourcing agent. It matches your brief to a hoodie manufacturer in Tirupur and coordinates sampling, production follow-up, quality control and shipment readiness. Certificates and factory approvals belong to the chosen factory.

What is the difference between fleece and French terry?

Fleece has a brushed, napped inner face and feels warmer. French terry has unbrushed loops inside and is lighter and more breathable. Read fleece vs French terry for how to choose.

How is hoodie shrinkage controlled?

Agree the wash-test method and tolerance in the tech pack, confirm whether the fabric is pre-shrunk, and measure a washed sample before bulk production. Shrinkage is checked again during quality control.

Can you source custom or private label hoodies?

Yes, subject to feasibility and order confirmation. Share your tech pack or references, plus label, hangtag, decoration and packing requirements.

Fleecewear sample review

Resolve the details that change a hoodie.

Two hoodies with the same fabric weight can still need different development work. A zip-through style introduces fastening and front-alignment decisions; a pullover needs its own neck-opening, hood and pocket review. Record the chosen construction in your brief rather than using a reference photograph as the only instruction.

Resolve the details that change a hoodie.
Fabric and inner finishSpecify the fabric composition, weight, structure and intended inner surface. Compare the sample with an approved hand-feel reference and record the appearance after the agreed care process. Confirm whether the hood lining and body fabric are meant to match or contrast.
Hood and necklineReview the hood depth, opening, panel construction and attachment at the neck while the sample is worn. State whether a lining, drawcord or other adjustment is part of the design. Record the approved details for the intended wearer and size range.
Rib and matching piecesReview cuff and hem dimensions alongside the body and sleeve measurements. Identify colour and hand-feel references for the rib, body fabric and any coordinating bottoms, including which components require matching approval.
Fastenings and decorationFor zip-through styles, identify zipper length, colour, puller and front finish. For printed or embroidered styles, show artwork position in relation to pockets, seams and the zipper. Review the decoration on the intended garment construction before closing approval.

Keep each approved fabric, trim and artwork reference with the style specification. If one component changes, identify which sample approvals need reviewing before bulk production. Use the sampling process to close those decisions and the quality checkpoints to follow them through production.

Fabric and weight

Fleece types and weights at a glance.

Choose the fleece type first, then the weight. Confirm both on a swatch before sampling.

Brushed fleeceWarm, soft napped inside. Typical for winter hoodies and sweatshirts.
French terryLooped inside, lighter and more breathable. Suits spring, autumn and activewear.
240 to 280 GSMLight hoodies and layering sweatshirts.
300 to 350 GSMMid-weight everyday hoodies.
380 GSM and aboveHeavyweight hoodies with a boxy, structured drape.

Check these first

The details that fail first on a hoodie.

Fleece garments rarely fail on the body fabric. They fail on the small, repeated stress points.

  • Rib cuff and hem recovery after repeated stretch and wash.
  • Pilling on the napped inside and at friction points.
  • Drawcord and eyelet attachment, and any restriction on cords in your market.
  • Pocket reinforcement and bar tacks at stress points.
  • Print and puff behaviour on the real fleece, checked with a strike-off.
