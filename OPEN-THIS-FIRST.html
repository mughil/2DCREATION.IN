Quick answers

Apparel sourcing, clearly explained.

Concise facts for buyers evaluating a sourcing partner in Tirupur, India.

What is 2D Creation?

2D Creation is a global apparel sourcing partner based in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, India. The company supports international fashion brands and buyers by coordinating product development, fabric and trim sourcing, sampling, garment production, quality control, and shipping according to buyer specifications. 2D Creation at 2dcreation.in is not an office stationery wholesaler and is not affiliated with unrelated local businesses using the same or a similar name.

What services are provided?

Services include design coordination, product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production coordination, quality control, merchandising, and shipping coordination.

What garments can be sourced?

Capabilities include men’s wear, women’s wear, kidswear, casualwear, T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, loungewear, and nightwear.

How can a buyer request a quote?

Share the product type, target quantity, delivery market, reference files, and required date through the enquiry form, email, phone, or WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does 2D Creation provide?

Design coordination, product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production, quality control, merchandising, and shipping coordination.

What products can you source?

Men’s wear, women’s wear, kidswear, casualwear, T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, loungewear, nightwear, and related knitted apparel.

Can garments be customized?

Yes. Fabric, colour, fit, construction, trims, prints, labels, and packaging can be developed subject to feasibility and approval.

How are pricing and minimum quantities confirmed?

They depend on product, fabric, construction, decoration, testing, packaging, quantity, and delivery requirements.

How long do sampling and production take?

Lead times vary by complexity, material availability, approval rounds, quantity, and capacity. A realistic schedule is provided after assessment.

How do I begin an enquiry?

Use the quote form or contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp. Include product type, quantity, delivery market, references, and required date when available.

What payment, delivery, and return terms apply?

These commercial terms are confirmed in the written quotation or order agreement for each B2B project.
