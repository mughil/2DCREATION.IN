Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy explains how 2D Creation handles information received through this website.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, work email, phone number, company information, project details, and any files you voluntarily submit through the enquiry form.

How we use information

We use submitted information to answer enquiries, assess sourcing requirements, prepare quotations, coordinate requested services, maintain business records, protect the website, and comply with applicable obligations.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 only after you allow usage analytics. When allowed, Google Analytics may process page views, referral information, approximate location, device and browser information, enquiry completion, and contact-link interactions. Analytics storage is denied until permission is granted, advertising storage remains disabled, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Analytics settings control. Google handles Analytics information under its privacy policy.

Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit. Links may open Google Maps, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or your configured email application, whose own privacy terms apply.

Storage and retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business relationship, legal obligations, dispute handling, or security. Do not submit sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent for your personal information. Verification may be required before a request is completed.

External links

We are not responsible for the content, availability, or privacy practices of external websites.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when services, technology, or legal requirements change. The updated date will be shown on this page.
