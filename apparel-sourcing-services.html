How sourcing moves

A clear six-stage workflow.

Milestones remain visible so decisions can be made before they become production delays.

  1. Review the brief

    Clarify product, market, quantity, quality, target and timing.

  2. Map feasibility

    Identify material, construction, development and capacity considerations.

  3. Develop samples

    Coordinate prototypes and revisions against consolidated comments.

  4. Confirm the order

    Align approved specifications, commercial terms and production inputs.

  5. Follow production

    Track milestones, quality observations and corrective actions.

  6. Prepare handover

    Confirm final packing, documentation and shipment readiness.

Common questions

Before starting an enquiry.

Can I enquire without a finished tech pack?

Yes. Share the clearest references you have and identify what still needs development. The team can then explain which specifications are required before sampling or costing.

How are minimum quantities and pricing confirmed?

They depend on fabric, colour, construction, decoration, trims, packaging and total order structure. They are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

How long will sampling and production take?

A realistic schedule is prepared after reviewing material availability, sample rounds, approval timing, order quantity and production capacity.

What you receive

The deliverables behind each stage.

A sourcing service is only useful if you can see what it produces. This is what you should expect to hold at each stage.

Brief reviewA written list of missing specifications, such as measurement points, GSM, trims and packing, before any factory is approached.
Factory matchA shortlist of factories that already make your product type, with the reason each one was chosen.
SamplingConsolidated comments for every sample round, and one signed-off reference sample kept as the production standard.
ProductionA milestone calendar covering fabric, cutting, sewing, finishing and packing, with delays flagged early.
QualityInspection reports at agreed stages, with photographs, measurements and a pass or fail against the agreed standard.
ShipmentPacking details, documentation inputs and a final handover checklist before dispatch.

Typical first orders are 300 to 500 pieces per style. We work with buyers in the UK, USA, Europe, Australia and other international markets. We reply to new briefs within 1 working day.

Choose the right route

Agent, factory or trading company.

These three are often confused, and the difference decides who is accountable when something goes wrong.

Sourcing agentDoes not own the factory. Matches your product to a suitable manufacturer and coordinates sampling, production and quality. 2D Creation works as a sourcing agent.
Direct factoryCheapest per unit for large, stable orders, but you manage development, quality and communication yourself.
Trading companyBuys and resells. Fast quotes on standard products, but the margin is hidden in the unit price.

Why a sourcing partner

Why international buyers use an apparel sourcing partner in Tirupur.

Tirupur has the knitwear supply chain close together, but a buyer overseas still has to find the right factory, align on a specification and stay on top of production from another time zone. A sourcing partner takes on that coordination.

01

One point of contact

Your comments, approvals and questions go through one team that consolidates them for each factory, so nothing is lost between sample rounds. Approvals are recorded in writing and the approved sample becomes the production reference.

02

Fabric and trims to your specification

We source fabric, rib, trims, labels and packing against your tech pack or reference garment, and confirm GSM, composition and finish on a swatch before sampling starts.

03

The right factory for the product

A T-shirt programme, a fleece hoodie and a baby bodysuit need different skills and machines. We match each product to a factory that already makes it, with typical first orders of 300 to 500 pieces per style.

04

Quality and export coordination

Inspections are planned against an agreed AQL standard, and we coordinate packing, documents and freight forwarding, with shipments usually leaving from Tuticorin or Chennai.
