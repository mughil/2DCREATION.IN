Sourcing from India

How international buyers source apparel from India.

India is one of the main places to source knitwear, and Tirupur (also spelt Tiruppur), in Tamil Nadu, is one of its main knitwear clusters. This page explains how an order runs, what overseas buyers should prepare, and where 2D Creation, a sourcing agent based in Tirupur, fits into the process.

Why India

Match the product to the cluster.

India’s apparel industry is organised in regional clusters, and each cluster is known for different products. Cotton knitwear is concentrated in Tirupur, winter knitwear in Ludhiana, and woven garments and fashion in the Delhi NCR region and Bengaluru.

For an overseas buyer, choosing the right cluster for the product usually matters more than choosing the country. A T-shirt programme, a fleece hoodie and a baby bodysuit need different skills and machines, so the first sourcing decision is which factory already makes your product type.

How an order runs

From brief to shipment, in six stages.

Milestones stay visible so decisions are made before they become production delays.

  1. Review the brief

    Clarify product, market, quantity, quality, target and timing, and list any missing specifications such as measurement points, GSM, trims and packing.

  2. Match the factory

    Shortlist factories that already make your product type, with the reason each was chosen. 2D Creation works as a sourcing agent and does not own the factories.

  3. Develop samples

    Coordinate prototypes, fit comments and approvals, and keep one signed-off reference sample. See product development and sampling.

  4. Confirm the order

    Align approved specifications, materials, commercial terms and production inputs. Pricing, quantities and lead times are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

  5. Follow production and quality

    Track fabric, cutting, sewing, finishing and packing milestones against agreed inspection stages. See apparel quality control.

  6. Coordinate export

    Confirm packing details, documents and freight forwarding. Quotations are commonly FOB, and shipments usually leave from Tuticorin or Chennai.

Your brief

What international buyers should provide.

A complete first brief shortens sampling. If a detail is undecided, mark it clearly so the next questions can be asked early.

  • Destination market and any labelling, safety or testing rules that apply there
  • Garment category, reference images, sketches or tech pack
  • Fabric direction: composition, GSM, structure and finish where known
  • Base size, size range, fit direction and intended customer
  • Target quantity by style, colour and size
  • Print, embroidery, wash, trims, labels, hangtags and packing requirements
  • Testing needs and any factory standards you require
  • Required sample date, required delivery date and target price if available
  • The person who approves comments, and the time zone you work in

Common questions

Sourcing apparel from India.

Is India a good country to source apparel from?

India has established apparel clusters that specialise by product, so the answer depends on what you buy. Tirupur is one of the main clusters for cotton knitwear such as T-shirts, hoodies and kidswear. Match the product to the cluster before choosing a partner. See the guide to sourcing agents and regions in India.

Which Indian city is best for T-shirts and hoodies?

Tirupur, in Tamil Nadu, is one of India’s main knitwear clusters and is where 2D Creation is based. Other clusters focus on other products, for example Ludhiana on winter knitwear and Delhi NCR and Bengaluru on woven garments.

How are orders shipped to international buyers?

Apparel quotations are commonly FOB, with the buyer arranging main freight. 2D Creation coordinates packing information, export documents and freight forwarding, and shipments usually leave from Tuticorin or Chennai. Import clearance is handled on the buyer’s side.

Do I need to visit India before placing an order?

It helps for a first large programme but is not mandatory. Factory profiles, video walk-throughs and inspection reports can be arranged, and you can visit once the relationship is established.

What is a typical first order?

Typical first orders are 300 to 500 pieces per style. Minimum quantities, pricing and lead times depend on fabric, construction, decoration and packaging, and are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

Ready to brief your first India order?

Share your style references, fabric direction, quantities by style, colour and size, destination market and required date. If a detail is undecided, mark it clearly.
