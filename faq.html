Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for apparel sourcing and development enquiries.

What services does 2D Creation provide?

We support design coordination, product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production, quality control, merchandising, and shipping coordination. Learn more about our apparel sourcing services.

What products can you source?

Capabilities include men’s wear, women’s wear, kidswear, casualwear, T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, loungewear, nightwear, and related knitted apparel, including our T-shirt and casualwear sourcing, hoodie and sweatshirt manufacturing, and womenswear and kidswear sourcing capabilities.

Can garments be customized?

Yes. Fabric, colour, fit, construction, trims, prints, labels, packaging, and other specifications can be developed subject to feasibility and written approval. See how our product development and sampling process works.

How are pricing and minimum order quantities confirmed?

Pricing and minimum quantities are confirmed after the product brief is reviewed. Include the quantity by style, colour and size, then list fabric, decoration, trims, testing and packing requirements. Separate confirmed specifications from options you want compared. A change in fabric, artwork or quantity can require a revised quotation, so compare quotations against the same brief and confirm the agreed version in writing.

How long do sampling and production take?

Timing depends on the completeness of the brief, material availability, sample revisions, buyer approvals, order quantity and production capacity. Share your required delivery date and destination at the enquiry stage, and distinguish that date from the date the goods need to be ready for dispatch. Sampling, bulk production and transport should be discussed as separate stages. The schedule is confirmed after assessment; there is no single lead time that applies to every product.

How do I start an enquiry?

Use the quote form or the contact options on the homepage. Share the garment type, reference images or tech pack, fabric direction, size range, quantity by style and colour, destination and required date. List any testing, labels or packing requirements already known. If a decision is still open, say so explicitly; a clear list of open questions is more useful than an assumed specification.

How are quality requirements managed?

Quality requirements are aligned with the approved sample, measurements, construction details and agreed inspection or testing plan before bulk production. Identify the reference version, acceptance criteria, review stages and decision authority for the order. Findings should remain linked to an action and recheck result where required. See the quality action record for the information a useful follow-up should contain.

What payment and delivery terms apply?

Payment, shipment, delivery, and commercial terms are confirmed in the written quotation or order agreement for each project.

Can an order be returned?

B2B return or remedy terms depend on the written order agreement, approved specifications, inspection findings, and applicable law. They are not governed by a general retail return policy.

What support is available after an enquiry?

A sourcing contact can coordinate clarification, development, approvals, production updates, quality checkpoints, and shipment readiness for accepted projects.

Can I enquire without a completed tech pack?

Yes. Start with the clearest references you have and identify the features that matter, including fit, fabric, artwork and intended use. A photograph cannot define every measurement or construction detail. Those gaps need to be resolved during product development and sampling before the garment is ready for production approval.

How should I send comments on a garment sample?

Identify the style, sample version, size and date. Group measurement, fit, material and artwork comments into one revision sheet, with photographs or marked-up references where helpful. State what is approved, what needs changing and who will confirm the next round. Use the sample comment checklist to keep revisions consistent.

Does an approved sample mean the order is ready for production?

Sample approval is one part of the handover. The final specification, materials, artwork, labels, packing, quantities, commercial terms and production plan also need to be aligned. Record any outstanding approvals and the responsible decision-maker. See the production and quality-control stages for the next steps.

Do you work with small or growing brands?

Yes. Newer and growing brands often benefit most from a single point of coordination. Typical first orders are 300 to 500 pieces per style. Send your brief and we will confirm what is realistic for your product.

Are you a factory or a sourcing agent?

We are a sourcing agent based in Tirupur, not a factory owner. We match each product to a suitable manufacturer and coordinate sampling, production follow-up and quality checks. Read more in what an apparel sourcing agent does.

How do you handle certificates such as OEKO-TEX or GOTS?

We ask the factory for the certificate copy and check the holder, scope, product class and validity before order confirmation. See how to verify an OEKO-TEX certificate.

What is AQL and which standard do you use?

AQL is the acceptable quality limit, which sets how many defects are tolerated in a sample drawn from a lot. We apply AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects. It is agreed in writing before production. See quality control and production follow-up.

How many sample rounds should I expect?

Plan on a first sample for fit and fabric, a second for corrections and decoration, and a pre-production sample from bulk fabric. See product development and sampling.

Which markets do you ship to?

We ship to the UK, USA, Europe, Australia and other international markets. Tell us your destination so labelling and testing requirements can be planned from the start.
